## Featured engineering research

### BESS Acoustic Intelligence (BAI)

Phase 1 is a Python research tool for discovering and collecting publicly accessible technical literature about noise emissions and mitigation at utility-scale battery energy storage systems. It supports configurable Tavily searches, access-respecting PDF discovery and downloads, SHA-256 deduplication, and SQLite provenance tracking.

See the [BESS Acoustic Intelligence project README](./Projects/BESS-Acoustic-Intelligence/README.md) for setup, usage, and the initial experiment results.
